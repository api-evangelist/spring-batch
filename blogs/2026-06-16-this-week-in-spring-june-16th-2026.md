A weekly roundup emphasizing urgency to upgrade to Spring Boot 4.1 due to security fixes addressing AI-driven CVEs. Major releases highlighted include Spring AI 2.0 GA with composable tool-calling, Spring Boot 4.1.0 with MongoDB batch metadata support, Spring Cloud/Data/Security patches, and Spring Tools 5.2.0; the cycle was delayed from May due to vulnerabilities found in AI-related components.
